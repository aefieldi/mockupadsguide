Step 1

Find the ads that brought in purchases

You're not guessing what to make. You're copying what already made you money. So first, pull your winners out of Meta.

  • My cutoff was more than 5 purchases. An ad needed at least 6 to make the list. Pick a cutoff that leaves you a short list, not a long one.
  • One row per creative. If the same image ran in three ads, that's one winner with three ad IDs.
  • Fix what's stale. My old ads still showed an old price. Catch that now so it doesn't get copied into 100 new ads.

Step 2

Turn your two best mockups into a creative brief

These are my two proven mockup ads. Before Claude made anything new, I had it tell me why these work.

The rules we carried into every new ad
  1. A top band with the hook. Black or white, serif headline, one phrase in a lavender gradient.
  2. The main thing in the center, on a laptop, with the other products fanned out around it and labelled.
  3. Real screenshots. People can read the actual product names, and that's what sells it.
  4. A FREE 7-DAY TRIAL badge you can't miss.
  5. A footer that says who it's for.
  6. Me, pointing at it.

  • Two ads before two hundred. If the first carousel is wrong, you fix one ad, not seventy slides.
  • Connector screens change. If a button isn't where I said, ask Claude "how do I connect Zernio to my Meta ad account right now?"
